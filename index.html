A PRACTICAL SOURCING BRIDGE

From a component drawing to a considered quotation.

Based in Eindhoven, CSOFTECH helps companies assess manufacturing options in India for selected components and engineering projects. We coordinate the initial requirement, technical questions and quotation process. Every enquiry is checked against the relevant supplier's actual capabilities before price, lead time or documentation is confirmed.

PRECISION COMPONENTS

Manufacturing options for custom parts.

For machine builders, automation companies and high-tech equipment projects. Suitability depends on the drawing, material, tolerances, quantity and inspection requirements.

01

CNC-machined components

Drawing-based parts such as plates, brackets, housings, blocks and other custom-machined components, subject to technical review.

02

Semiconductor tooling & fixtures

Selected tooling components, fixtures and mechanical parts for semiconductor and high-tech equipment applications.

03

Automation & machine parts

Custom mechanical components for industrial automation, special-purpose machinery and equipment builds.

04

Prototype & production batches

Enquiries for prototype, small, medium or larger quantities can be reviewed against available capacity and project needs.

05

Castings & forgings

Manufacturing enquiries involving cast or forged components, including any required machining, subject to supplier review.

06

Fabrication & assembly

Selected fabrication and assembly requirements can also be explored where the scope and specifications are a suitable fit.

No component is quoted until its key requirements and the supplier's manufacturing capability have been reviewed. Tolerances, inspection records, certificates and lead times are confirmed individually for each enquiry.

ABOUT CSOFTECH

A local point of contact for cross-border engineering enquiries.

CSOFTECH is based in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, with connections to engineering and manufacturing capabilities in India. Our role is to understand the customer's requirement, help organise technical and commercial discussions, and make sure key assumptions are checked before a quotation or delivery commitment is made.

Manufacturing is performed by the relevant supplier, not by CSOFTECH itself. Supplier identity, capability, inspection documentation and commercial terms are handled transparently as a specific opportunity progresses. CSOFTECH does not claim exclusive representation of any manufacturer.
