Main positioning
- CSOFTECH is presented as a Netherlands-based local contact for engineering enquiries and sourcing coordination.
- The website describes selected precision CNC-machined components and related manufacturing options through sources in India.
- It does not name Foreaxis Technology or claim an official/exclusive partnership.
- Manufacturing capability, inspection documentation, tolerances, prices and lead times are stated as subject to review per enquiry.

Before publishing
1. Confirm you are comfortable with all capability descriptions and the wording about CSOFTECH's role.
2. Verify the Formspree endpoint and test that the form reaches the intended inbox.
3. Confirm info@csoftech.nl is monitored. This address is also shown as a direct contact option.
4. Review legal/privacy requirements for the contact form; add a privacy notice and any required consent wording.
5. Confirm with the relevant manufacturing source which capabilities, documents and lead times can actually be offered before quoting to customers.
6. Check the website on desktop and mobile and then commit/push the files to GitHub Pages.
